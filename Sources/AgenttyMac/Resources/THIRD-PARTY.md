# Bundled third-party files

| File | What | Version | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mermaid.min.js` | [Mermaid](https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid), draws diagrams from text | 11.4.1 | MIT |

`mermaid.min.js` is the unmodified build from
`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mermaid@11.4.1/dist/mermaid.min.js`. It is
shipped in the app so diagrams render without a network connection.
