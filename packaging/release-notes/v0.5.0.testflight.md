AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version works with AgentTerm 0.4 on the Mac; what is new needs 0.5 there: https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.5.0

- A session looks right the moment you open it: the Mac sends the screen as it is, laid out for its current size, with 10,000 lines above it. An agent's screen no longer shows half drawn until it draws again.
- On its side: in landscape the terminal takes the screen, with the title, the keys and the message field in one row.
- A button above the keyboard hides it.
- Waiting is shown: the app says what it is loading, and after ten seconds that it is taking longer than usual, with Try Again.
- Start at Login, in the Settings tab: the Mac's host starts when you log in there, so the iPhone reaches the Mac after it restarts without the app being opened first.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.5 first, and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. Open a session where an agent is already running: the screen should be complete at once. Scroll up and check the earlier lines read right.
3. Turn the phone on its side in a session, type, and send a message.
4. Bring up the keyboard and hide it with the button above it.
5. Settings tab: turn on Start at Login, restart the Mac, log in, and connect from the iPhone without opening AgentTerm on the Mac.

What to tell me

Anything that looks wrong in the terminal (stray characters, a wrong size, a cursor in the wrong place, a key that does nothing), above all right after opening a session. Send feedback from TestFlight, or on Discord: https://discord.gg/Ksagmsd6j
