The fourth beta of AgentTerm 0.3.0. The apps are the same as in beta.3; what is new is how they reach you: this build goes to everyone who joined through the public TestFlight link, and releases are now announced on Discord.

AgentTerm 0.3.0 speaks the same protocol as 0.2.x (version 5), so a Mac and an iPhone on 0.2.x or 0.3.x work together. Coming from 0.1.x, update both apps.

## New in 0.3.0

- **Keep the Mac awake.** A switch in the Mac's Settings, and in the iPhone app's menu, keeps the Mac from going to sleep by itself while a session is open, so agents keep working and the phone can still reach them. Off by default.
- **Resumed sessions show their name.** A session started by resuming a conversation is listed under the conversation's title.
- **Crash reports and usage statistics, with your say.** The app asks once whether it may send crash reports; nothing is sent until you agree. Counts of which features are used are on by default and one switch turns them off. Never what your terminals show, your prompts, file names or folders. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Fixed in 0.3.0

- The diff tab closed when another folder's session was shown.
- On the iPhone a diff could not be scrolled.

## Install

- **Mac:** `brew tap manhpham90vn/tap`, `brew trust manhpham90vn/tap`, `brew install --cask agentterm` (or `brew upgrade --cask agentterm`), or the disk image below.
- **iPhone:** [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/Ny2JKHUp)
