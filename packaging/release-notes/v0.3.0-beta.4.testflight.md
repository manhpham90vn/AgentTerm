AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. It needs AgentTerm 0.2 or later on the Mac: https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.3.0

- Keep Mac Awake: a switch in the menu (the round button at the top right) keeps the Mac from sleeping while a session is open.
- A session started by resuming a conversation shows the conversation's name.
- Diffs scroll, down and sideways.
- Privacy: the app asks once about crash reports. The two switches are under Privacy in the menu.

Please try

1. Pair: on the Mac click Pair iPhone, then scan the code here.
2. Start a session in a folder, type in the Message field and send. Leave the session and open it again: the input should be clean.
3. Touch and hold a session: pin it, put it in a group, drag it onto another. The Mac's tabs should follow.
4. Open "Git, History, Files" of a folder with changes, open a change and scroll its diff.
5. Turn on Keep Mac Awake, leave a session open and check the Mac is still reachable after its usual sleep time.

What to tell me

Anything that looks wrong in the terminal (stray characters, a wrong size, a key that does nothing), and anything that differs between the Mac and the iPhone. Send feedback from TestFlight, or on Discord: https://discord.gg/Ksagmsd6j
