AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version needs AgentTerm 0.6 on the Mac, with its host restarted: https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.6.0

- Each session says what its agent is doing: working, waiting for you, or done.
- While you use the Mac from the iPhone: a banner, and a notification just after you leave the app, when an agent finished or needs an answer; answer from the notification.
- A Live Activity of the agents at work while no one is at the Mac.
- Schedules, in Settings: an agent started with a prompt at a time; the runs going on and those that are over.
- Long sessions scroll smoothly, and their earlier lines load as you scroll up.
- A session takes the iPhone's size by itself when you come back to it.
- Text files over 2 MB open with Open Anyway.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.6 first, and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. Start an agent and give it a task, leave the app: a notification should come when it finishes or asks something. Answer from it.
3. Turn on the Live Activity in Settings, step away from the Mac, and watch the lock screen while an agent works.
4. Open a long session and scroll up past the top: "Loading earlier lines" should show and the lines come without the screen jumping.
5. Settings > Schedules: make one for a few minutes ahead and see it run.

What to tell me

What did not work, what was slow, and anything that read wrong.
