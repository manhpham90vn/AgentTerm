AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version needs AgentTerm 0.4 on the Mac: https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.4.0

- Push and pull: in a folder's Git tab, Pull and Push act on the branch that is checked out. A branch that is not on a remote yet shows Publish Branch.
- Commit messages written by an agent: the sparkles button next to the commit message asks an agent on the Mac to write it. Nothing is committed until you commit. The agent, the model and the prompt are under Commit Messages in the Settings tab.
- Diffs in two panels: the button at the top right of a diff shows before and after side by side. Turn the phone on its side for room.
- The app opens on the list of your Macs; pick one to go in.
- Three tabs in place of one long menu: Sessions, History, Settings.
- Above the sessions: processor, memory, network speed, temperature (°C or °F), battery and uptime of the Mac you are connected to.
- Scroll back much further in a session: 20,000 lines, and the last 4 MB of what happened before you opened it.
- SSH: the hosts of the Mac's SSH configuration, next to the agents. The session runs on the Mac.
- Keep Mac Awake has options: turn off by itself after 1 to 8 hours, keep the display on too, only while plugged in.
- Fixed on the Mac: opening the app while an agent was already running could show a broken terminal.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.4 first, and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. In a folder with a remote: stage a change, tap the sparkles button, read the message, commit, then Push.
3. Check the figures above the sessions against the Mac's own.
4. If your Mac has hosts in ~/.ssh/config: tap SSH and open one.
5. Settings tab: set Keep Mac Awake to turn off after 1 hour and check the Mac's Settings show the same.
