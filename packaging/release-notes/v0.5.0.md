AgentTerm 0.5.0 still speaks protocol version 6, so it works with 0.4 on the other device. What is new needs 0.5 on both, and a host of 0.5: after updating the Mac app, restart the host when the app offers to.

## New

- **Use another Mac from this one.** A Mac can pair with another Mac the way an iPhone does, and then shows that Mac's sessions, folders, changes, history and files in its own window. Open Pair Device on the other Mac, copy the link under the code, and paste it under Pair with Another Mac in the menu at the top of the window, to the left of the agents. That menu says which Mac is on screen, in blue for this one and orange for another, and switches between them. Agents and shells started there run on that Mac. Both Macs have to be on the same Tailscale network.
- **A session looks right the moment you open it.** A device that opens a session is sent the screen as it is, laid out for the size the session has now, with the 10,000 lines above it. A full screen agent no longer shows a half drawn screen until it draws again, and what was written while the session was narrower or wider no longer reads broken further up.
- **Start at login.** Settings > General on the Mac, and the Settings tab on the iPhone, can start the host when you log in to the Mac. After the Mac restarts, the iPhone reaches it without the app being opened there first.
- **The iPhone on its side.** In landscape the terminal takes the screen: the title, the keys and the message field share one row instead of leaving a few lines of terminal between them.
- **A button to hide the keyboard.** While the keyboard is up on the iPhone, a button above it puts it away.
- **Waiting is shown.** Wherever the app waits for the Mac (a session, a diff, the history, a file) it says what it is loading, and after ten seconds that it is taking longer than usual, with a way to try again.

The button to pair is now Pair Device, since it pairs an iPhone or another Mac.

## Fixed

- On the Mac, ending a session from its tab or the sidebar asks first. Before, one click ended an agent in the middle of its work.
- The Mac app can be used without a pointer: Settings switches, changed files and their Stage buttons, tabs, conversations and files are named for VoiceOver, and what was only in a right click menu (End Session, Close Other Tabs, Hand Off, Rename, Move to Trash) is offered as an action.
