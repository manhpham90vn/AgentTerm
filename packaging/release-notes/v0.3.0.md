AgentTerm 0.3.0 speaks the same protocol as 0.2.x (version 5), so a Mac and an iPhone on 0.2.x or 0.3.x work together. Coming from 0.1.x, update both apps.

## New

- **Crash reports and usage statistics, with your say.** The app asks once whether it may send crash reports; nothing is sent until you agree. Counts of which features are used are on by default and one switch turns them off. Never what your terminals show, your prompts, file names or folders. The switches are in Settings > Privacy on the Mac and under Privacy in the iPhone app's menu. See PRIVACY.md for exactly what is sent.
- **Resumed sessions show their name.** A session started by resuming a conversation is listed under the conversation's title, like any other.

## Fixed

- The diff tab closed when another folder's session was shown. It now stays, and comes back with its own folder.

## For those who build from source

- A build from source, and AgentTerm Dev, send nothing: they contain no reporting service.
- `make` lists the common tasks; `make lint` and `make format` use swift-format.

## Install

- **Mac:** `brew tap manhpham90vn/tap`, `brew trust manhpham90vn/tap`, `brew install --cask agentterm`, or the disk image below.
- **iPhone:** TestFlight, https://testflight.apple.com/join/Ny2JKHUp
